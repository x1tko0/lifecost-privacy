LifeCost

Price In Hours — Privacy Policy

Short version

Your income and your decisions stay on your phone. LifeCost has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash-reporting SDKs. The app does not request the Internet permission.

Data the app stores

Only what you enter yourself:

  • Income profiles: amount, period, working hours and days, currency, and optional commute time, unpaid hours and work-related costs used for the “real hourly wage”.
  • Decisions: prices you checked with an optional name and category, and whether you bought, skipped or put the item on hold.
  • Recurring expenses, savings goals, your own example prices and app settings.

This data:

  • is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage;
  • is never sent to the developer or any third party;
  • is deleted with “Delete all data” in Settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Financial information

Income is sensitive. LifeCost uses it only on your device to convert prices into hours and to show your own totals. The app does not connect to banks, does not process payments (except the Pro purchase through Google Play) and gives no financial advice.

Text from other apps

When you select text in another app and choose “In hours of life”, or share text to LifeCost, the app reads only that text, on your device, to find a price in it. The text is not stored unless you save a decision, and it is never sent anywhere.

Backups

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include LifeCost's data in your device backup to your Google account and restore it on a new phone. This is controlled by Android and Google (Google Privacy Policy), not by us, and you can turn it off in system settings.
  • Backup file and CSV. “Save backup” and “Export CSV” create a file only where you choose to save it. We never see it.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information, accounts or sign-in.
  • No location, contacts, camera, photos or files (except a backup file you pick to import).
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, crash-reporting SDKs or tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising.

Google Play services

The app talks only to the Google Play Store app on your phone:

  • Google Play Billing — the one-time “LifeCost Pro” purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google; the app never sees your payment details.
  • In-app updates and in-app review, provided by Google Play.

Notifications

The end-of-pause reminder, the weekly recap and the one-time “Pro trial ends tomorrow” notice are scheduled on your device. Notification text can appear on your lock screen — you can turn them off in Settings or in Android's settings.

Sharing

“Share” creates an image on your device and shares it only when you tap it and choose where to send it.

Children

LifeCost is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
